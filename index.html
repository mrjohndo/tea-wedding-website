Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are kids welcome?

Yes — just let us know their ages and how many when you RSVP. Note: Kids will have the same menu at the Tea Ceremony and a different menu at the Wedding reception.

Are we "kicking' on after tea ceremony & wedding ceremony?

There will potentially be a lot of alcoholic beverages consumed at both ceremonies. If people still have the urge to continue celebrating then this is typically referred to as "Tăng 2" (Second level) and will usually include activities such as Karaoke.

What is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by October 1st so we can finalize arrangements with our venues.

What should I wear to the tea ceremony?

Semi-formal — a comfortable dress, áo dài, shirt, or polo works well. It will be warm and humid, and the event is outdoors (under cover).

What should I wear to the wedding ceremony?

Semi-formal or cocktail attire. Light, breathable fabrics work well in the Vietnamese climate.

Will there be transportation to the tea ceremony?

Ideally John's family & friends will travel together. We will try to organise buses — details to be confirmed.

How do I get to the wedding reception?

Booking by Grab (similar to Uber) will be approx. 5–10 min for those staying in Thao Dien or Vinhomes Central Park.
